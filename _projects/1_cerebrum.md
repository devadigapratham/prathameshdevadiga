End-to-end LLM system with distributed FSDP/DDP training, Flash Attention v2, and Mixture-of-Experts support. Includes **Mixture-of-Refusals (MoR)**, a safety routing mechanism achieving 2–3× speedup on safe queries. vLLM inference with quantization, speculative decoding, and prefix caching, deployed on Kubernetes.
